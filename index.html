Why it happens

A readable EPUB can still fail Kindle’s checks.

Before you upload

A few honest answers.

Can you guarantee my cover will appear on every Kindle?+

No. We can repair detectable problems inside the EPUB, but Kindle apps, device firmware, download state and Amazon’s conversion service can also affect thumbnails. If your EPUB is already healthy, we’ll tell you instead of making unnecessary changes.

Why did Send to Kindle reject my EPUB?+

Common causes include invalid package XML, a malformed container, missing manifest resources, broken spine references or incorrect ZIP packaging. The diagnostic names the blocker it can prove without guessing.

Do you upload or keep my ebook?+

No. The EPUB is opened and rebuilt locally by your browser. It is not sent to FixMyEPUB or stored on a server.

Will this change the text or formatting?+

The cover repair only changes package metadata and, when necessary, the selected cover resource. It does not rewrite chapters or alter the prose.

Does this remove DRM?+

No. FixMyEPUB is for DRM-free EPUB files you are allowed to modify. Encrypted or DRM-protected books are not supported.

What should I do after downloading the repaired EPUB?+

Remove the old personal document from your Kindle library, then upload the repaired copy through Amazon’s Send to Kindle service. Duplicate copies may retain the old thumbnail.
